# Banking Customer & Financial Analytics Using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing banking customer data to understand customer demographics, banking relationships, loyalty classifications, and financial account balances.

Using Python and its data analysis libraries, the project explores customer information and account-level metrics to identify patterns, understand customer segments, and generate insights that can support business decision-making.

The objective is to transform raw banking data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), statistical analysis, and data visualization.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Banks manage large amounts of customer and financial data, but raw data alone does not provide a clear understanding of customer behavior or banking relationships.

Without proper analysis, it can be difficult to understand which customer segments hold higher account balances, how customer loyalty varies across different groups, and how financial relationships differ based on customer demographics and banking services.

The business needs a structured way to analyze customer information, identify patterns in account balances, and better understand its customer base.

## 💡 Project Objectives

* Analyze customer demographics, including age, nationality, and occupation.
* Understand the distribution of customers across different loyalty classifications.
* Examine banking relationships and customer contact information.
* Analyze balances across bank deposits, checking accounts, savings accounts, and foreign currency accounts.
* Identify patterns in customer segments and their financial relationships.
* Create visualizations to communicate findings clearly.
* Support data-driven decisions related to customer segmentation and banking services.

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains banking customer information and financial account details.

| Column Name              | Description                                      |

| Joined Bank              | Date or period when the customer joined the bank |

| Loyalty Classification   | Customer loyalty category                        |
